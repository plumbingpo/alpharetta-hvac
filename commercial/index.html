Restaurant Services

Comprehensive Food-Service HVAC Systems

Restaurants and food-service businesses have climate demands that ordinary equipment can't meet. Kitchens generate enormous heat loads while dining rooms need to stay comfortable, exhaust hoods pull out conditioned air that makeup air units must replace, and a busy Friday night pushes rooftop units to their limit. Alpharetta HVAC specializes in food-service HVAC, including rooftop package units, split systems, correct tonnage and airflow sizing, makeup air balancing against hood exhaust, and scheduled service that works around your hours.

Comfort and air balance are critical concerns for any food-service operator. A kitchen running negative pressure pulls dining-room air the wrong way, drags outdoor air through every door, and makes staff miserable during peak service. Our work keeps your system delivering the temperatures and airflow your operation depends on, with documented service records that show a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors. We provide detailed reports and service logs after each visit, along with the documentation many commercial leases require.

Equipment replacements and maintenance schedules are planned with owners, managers, and kitchen leads, so everything fits your operating schedule. If a job can't be done during service, we do it after close, keeping the kitchen running and the dining room comfortable throughout.

Safe Installation

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to maintain safe, comfortable conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected HVAC system can result in carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and costly water damage from clogged condensate lines. Alpharetta HVAC provides commercial installations and inspections that cover heat exchanger integrity, proper venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge verification, condensate drainage protection, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

Consistent professional service protects your people and your property, and following through on our recommendations helps keep small issues from becoming liabilities. It also builds a paper trail. We maintain service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent visits for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms. That record shows landlords, insurers, and auditors that you've done your due diligence.
