Heating and cooling systems rarely fail politely, and in Alpharetta, GA, a breakdown tends to land on the hottest afternoon or the coldest night of the year. The tricky part is how many different failures produce the same symptom. A system that runs but never reaches temperature might be fighting a weak run capacitor, a pitted contactor, a low refrigerant charge, a dirty flame sensor, or simply a filter so clogged the blower is starving for air. A system that will not start at all could be a tripped float switch on a backed-up condensate drain, a failed transformer, a bad thermostat, or a control board that has stopped sequencing the equipment.

HVAC Repair Prevention Tips

Smart maintenance is almost always cheaper and easier than an early replacement. Start with these practical measures our team relies on:

  • Clear the condensate drain every spring so algae never backs it up and shuts the system down
  • Replace the air filter every one to three months to keep airflow strong across the blower and coil
  • Give the system a rest during extreme weather by setting the thermostat a few degrees closer to outdoors
  • Keep supply and return registers open and unblocked in every room
  • Call about unusual noises early, since a squealing bearing costs far less to fix before the motor seizes
  • Trim shrubs and clear leaves and grass away from the outdoor unit so the coil gets full airflow
  • Schedule professional maintenance twice a year, before cooling season and before heating season
